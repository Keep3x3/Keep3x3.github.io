FAQ

How do I reset my progress?
Delete and reinstall the app — all local data will be cleared.

How does the Daily Challenge work?
Every day, all players get the same puzzle. Your time is submitted to the Game Center leaderboard so you can see how you rank.

Why do I see ads?
Ads help keep Pulse free for everyone. You can deny tracking permission for less personalized ads.
