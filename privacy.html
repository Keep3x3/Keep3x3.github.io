Privacy Policy — Pulse

Effective date: April 25, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Pulse mobile application ("Pulse", "the app", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use it on iOS.

By using Pulse you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Who we are

Pulse is developed and maintained by Bastien Piechon, based in France.

Contact email: bastien.piechon@icloud.com

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information collected automatically

When you launch Pulse, the following information may be processed:

  • Device identifier (IDFA) — only if you grant tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt on first launch. Used by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) to serve more relevant ads.
  • General device data — device model, operating system version, language, country, mobile network type. Collected by Google AdMob to deliver ads and prevent fraud.
  • Ad interaction events — impressions, clicks, video views. Collected by Google AdMob.
  • Diagnostic data — anonymous crash reports and performance metrics, used to improve app stability.

2.2 Information you provide

  • Player name and country — set in the Profile screen and stored locally on your device. Used only to label your scores on the Game Center leaderboard.
  • Game progress — best times, unlocked themes, saved random seeds. Stored locally on your device only.

2.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, or any contact details.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not access your photos, microphone, camera, contacts, calendar, or location.

3. How we use information

The data above is used solely to:

  1. Display advertisements that help keep Pulse free.
  2. Submit your daily challenge time to the Game Center leaderboard so other players can see how they rank.
  3. Save your progress and preferences locally on your device.
  4. Send you a daily local notification reminding you about the Daily Challenge (only if you grant notification permission).

4. Third-party services

Pulse integrates with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs the data they collect:

  • Google AdMob — for serving advertisements. https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Game Center — for the daily leaderboard, managed by Apple. https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We do not share your data with any other third party.

5. Your choices

  • You can deny tracking permission when the App Tracking Transparency prompt appears. AdMob will still serve ads, but they may be less relevant.
  • You can disable Game Center in your iOS settings.
  • You can delete the app at any time. All locally stored data (progress, preferences, saved seeds) is removed when you uninstall.

6. Children's privacy

Pulse is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. The advertising partner (AdMob) is configured to comply with applicable children's privacy regulations.

7. Your rights (GDPR — European Economic Area)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data processed about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. by denying tracking permission).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at bastien.piechon@icloud.com. We respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (CNIL in France: https://www.cnil.fr/en/contact-cnil).

8. Data retention

  • Local data on your device persists until you delete the app.
  • Anonymous data sent to AdMob is retained per Google's retention policy.
  • Game Center scores are retained per Apple's retention policy.

We do not store any of your data on our own servers — Pulse has no backend infrastructure beyond the third-party services listed above.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
